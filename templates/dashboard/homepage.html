Overview

What exactly is a BRSR filing?

The Business Responsibility & Sustainability Report is a standardised disclosure that listed companies file alongside their annual report. It replaces the older, largely qualitative Business Responsibility Report (BRR) with a structured, quantifiable account of ESG performance.

It's built on the National Guidelines on Responsible Business Conduct (NGRBC) — nine principles issued by the Ministry of Corporate Affairs — and asks companies to report against defined indicators rather than free-form narrative, making disclosures comparable across sectors and years.

  • Introduced and mandated by SEBI (Securities and Exchange Board of India)
  • Mandatory for the top 1,000 listed companies by market capitalisation
  • Built on the nine NGRBC principles — ethics, environment, human rights and more
  • Filed as part of the annual report, from FY 2022–23 onward

Why It Matters

Why companies use the BRSR framework

Beyond compliance, BRSR gives regulators, investors and companies themselves a shared, auditable language for sustainability performance.

Regulatory compliance

Meets SEBI's mandatory disclosure norms for listed companies, avoiding penalties and filing delays.

Investor confidence

Gives investors decision-useful, comparable ESG data instead of scattered sustainability claims.

Risk visibility

Surfaces environmental and social risks in the reporting cycle, before they turn into financial ones.

Transparency & trust

Builds credibility with regulators, customers and communities through verifiable, public disclosure.

Global alignment

Maps cleanly onto GRI, BRSR Core and other international ESG standards for cross-border reporting.

Long-term value

Turns sustainability commitments into a trackable, year-on-year record rather than one-off claims.

The NGRBC Framework

The nine principles behind BRSR

Section C of every report is organised around these nine principles, each covering a distinct area of responsible conduct.

P1

Ethics, transparency & accountability

Businesses conduct themselves ethically and are accountable to stakeholders.

P2

Safe & sustainable goods

Products and services are safe and sustainable across their full lifecycle.

P3

Employee wellbeing

Businesses respect and promote the wellbeing of all employees, including value-chain workers.

P4

Stakeholder interests

Businesses respect the interests of, and are responsive to, all stakeholders.

P5

Human rights

Businesses respect and promote human rights across their operations.

P6

Environmental protection

Businesses respect and make efforts to protect and restore the environment.

P7

Responsible advocacy

Policy engagement and advocacy are conducted in a responsible manner.

P8

Inclusive growth

Businesses support equitable development and inclusive growth for all.

P9

Consumer value

Businesses engage with and provide value to consumers responsibly.

Common Questions

Frequently asked

Who is required to file a BRSR?

SEBI mandates BRSR filing for the top 1,000 listed companies by market capitalisation, as part of their annual report from FY 2022–23 onward. Other listed entities may file it voluntarily.

How is BRSR different from the old BRR?

The earlier Business Responsibility Report was largely descriptive and qualitative. BRSR replaces it with quantifiable, indicator-based disclosures that can be compared across companies and years.

What's the difference between Essential and Leadership indicators?

Essential indicators are mandatory for every applicable company. Leadership indicators are recommended, more advanced disclosures for companies pursuing stronger ESG performance.

Does BRSR replace financial reporting?

No. BRSR sits alongside financial statements as a non-financial, ESG-focused disclosure — it doesn't substitute for statutory financial reporting requirements.
